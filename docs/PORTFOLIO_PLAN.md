# Personal portfolio plan

Stocks and ETFs, entered manually. The password-protected Sites app saves the portfolio across devices; GitHub Pages keeps a separate browser-local copy. No broker connections, public accounts, sharing, or community features.

1. **Foundation — complete:** reporting currency and start date, opening positions, manual transactions, validation, backup/restore, historical entry, ticker suggestions, and actual EUR cash settlement.
2. **Calculations — complete:** historical EUR valuation, realized/unrealized gains, approximate cash-flow-adjusted monthly returns, historical FX, and matched benchmark comparison. Performance has its own page; research-pick outcomes remain separate. See README for coverage and calculation limits.
3. **Organization and analytics — complete for available data:** synced groups and position notes, EUR allocation charts by holding/group/asset type, best/worst unrealized EUR performers and direct-position concentration. The ETF report adds full published VWCE company exposure, country/sector breakdowns and overlap with direct stocks.
4. **Six core reports — complete for available data:** separate allocation, ETF exposure, portfolio performance, individual investment performance, contributions/withdrawals, and transaction pages, with CSV exports and applicable filters. VWCE and verified accumulating-share-class listings use Vanguard's full dated stock list, reconcile all issuer pages, combine issuer share classes/ADRs and leave non-stock/residual fund value separate. The report is searchable/paged and refreshes automatically. Other ETF families still need verified provider adapters; same-fund listings are identified in supported position overlap. Investment capital gains include held and sold positions; cash dividends remain separate in portfolio performance.
5. **Portfolio insights and ChatGPT summary — complete:** calculation-based explanations of EUR gains, allocation, monthly returns, income and fees, with dated supporting figures. A reviewable summary can be copied into ChatGPT using an existing account; notes are opt-in. No AI API or automatic external sharing. Company financial/news research remains outside this version.
6. **Remaining reports — in progress:** dividend cash-flow forecast complete, using manually entered gross per-share amounts, payment dates and repeat intervals. It uses current shares and dated FX, saves assumptions across devices and in backups, and exports monthly payment estimates. Retirement scenarios and annual summary remain planned.

Each feature receives appropriate validation and a separate commit/push.
