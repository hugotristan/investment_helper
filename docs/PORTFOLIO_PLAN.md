# Personal portfolio plan

Stocks and ETFs, entered manually. The password-protected Sites app saves the portfolio across devices; GitHub Pages keeps a separate browser-local copy. No broker connections, public accounts, sharing, or community features.

1. **Foundation — complete:** reporting currency and start date, opening positions, manual transactions, validation, backup/restore, historical entry, ticker suggestions, and actual EUR cash settlement.
2. **Calculations — complete:** historical EUR valuation, realized/unrealized gains, approximate cash-flow-adjusted monthly returns, historical FX, and matched benchmark comparison. Performance has its own page; research-pick outcomes remain separate. See README for coverage and calculation limits.
3. **Organization and analytics — complete for available data:** synced groups and position notes, EUR allocation charts by holding/group/asset type, best/worst unrealized EUR performers and direct-position concentration. Full ETF overlap still needs complete dated fund holdings; the reports phase adds partial VWCE company exposure.
4. **Six core reports — complete for available data:** separate allocation, ETF exposure, portfolio performance, individual investment performance, contributions/withdrawals, and transaction pages, with CSV exports and applicable filters. VWCE exposure uses Vanguard's dated top ten securities, combines issuer share classes/ADRs, and leaves other constituents unreported. Complete fund overlap and exposure for other ETFs still need verified holdings datasets. Investment capital gains include held and sold positions; cash dividends remain separate in portfolio performance.
5. **AI analyst — implementation prepared; activation pending:** dedicated page, server-built portfolio evidence, optional position notes, validated numeric references and dated citations, authenticated provider endpoint and request limits. The OpenAI Developers credential setup and a live model check are still needed before calling this phase complete. Company financial/news research remains outside this first version.
6. **Remaining reports — planned:** future dividend cash flow, retirement scenarios, and annual summary, with assumptions and missing data visible.

Each feature receives appropriate validation and a separate commit/push.
