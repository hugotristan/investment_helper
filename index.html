Data sources: Yahoo Finance intraday and one-year chart data plus a 260-source trusted universe scanned through GDELT live news JSON and direct RSS feeds where available, including official SEC, Fed, BLS, BEA, FTC, EIA, and ECB feeds. The app also checks broad-market proxies including SPY, QQQ, IWM, VTI, TLT, HYG, LQD, GLD, USO, UUP, and VIX to estimate market regime. Buy, sell, hold, and forecast outputs are probabilistic model signals only. This app does not place trades or guarantee returns.
