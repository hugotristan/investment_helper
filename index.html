    Monthly performance

    Gains and costs
    How this is calculated

    Monthly returns adjust for the size and timing of deposits and withdrawals using Modified Dietz, assuming end-of-day cash movements. The overall return links the monthly results. It is an approximation; large deposits or withdrawals can make it less accurate.

    Prices use daily closes; historical currency conversion uses the latest available ECB reference rate on or before each date, up to seven calendar days earlier. Recorded EUR trade settlements supply purchase cost and sale proceeds. Missing history and unresolved splits leave affected calculations unavailable.

    Dividends and fees affect your return when recorded. The SPY comparison is a price return in EUR and excludes benchmark dividends. Opening positions start performance at their market value on the tracking date; earlier performance and funding are not reconstructed. Net deposits are only the funding you recorded.

    How returns are measured

    Data and limitations

    Prices come from Yahoo Finance. Annual financials use SEC companyfacts, with Yahoo annual statements as a fallback. Earnings dates come from a limited set of issuer announcements. News is collected through GDELT and available RSS feeds. Scores use fixed rules; they are not probabilities. Missing or stale price history cannot produce signals. The app does not place trades.
